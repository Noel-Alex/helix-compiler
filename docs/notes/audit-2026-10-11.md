## Measurement limits

Browser measurements compare the same source with different timing scopes. Interpreter samples include parsing, checking, execution and checksum. Compiled samples time the emitted module's execution after compilation, excluding final-array checksum. Worker startup and transport are excluded. These numbers are browser evidence, not native or parallel speedups.

Recorded native campaign files and their source identity were left unchanged: this pass does not alter compiler semantics or regenerate performance claims. Large bundled workloads skip live browser execution and retain their recorded evidence.
